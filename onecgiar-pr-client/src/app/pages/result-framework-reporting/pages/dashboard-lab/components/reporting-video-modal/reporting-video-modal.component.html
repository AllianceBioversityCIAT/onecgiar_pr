Instant Keyword Search Bar

Located in the sticky reporting toolbar. Type any keyword (e.g. breeding, market, HLO4, or indicator code). Matching cards and titles are dynamically highlighted with a yellow background, and an indicator match counter displays the exact number of matching KPIs.

Quick Typology Chips

Filter your view with one click by result typology: Knowledge Products, Innovation Development, Policy Changes, or Outcomes. Each chip indicates how many planned indicators belong to that typology.

1

Locate your Area of Work (AoW)

Browse through the 5–8 Areas of Work configured for your Science Program. Click the header or press Space to expand the card.

2

Expand the High-Level Output (HLO)

Inside the AoW, indicators are grouped by HLO or Outcome milestones. Click the HLO group to reveal its planned indicators and targets.

3

Click "Report" to open the Indicator Drawer

On the indicator row, click the Report (or Continue) button. A sliding aside drawer opens on the right side of the screen without losing your place in the list.

4

Complete the Dynamic Result Form

Fill out the form fields tailored to the indicator's Result Type. For Knowledge Products, search and link the CGSpace handle; for Innovation Development, input the readiness level; for Policy Changes, declare the policy stage.

5

Save and Track Real-Time Progress

Save your progress. The target ratio, progress percentages, and status badge (Not Started → In Progress → Achieved) update immediately across the entire Science Program dashboard.
