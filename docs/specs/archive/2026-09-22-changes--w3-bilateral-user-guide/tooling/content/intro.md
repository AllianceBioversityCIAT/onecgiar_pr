PRMS's bilateral module is where Center reporters submit results funded through Window 3 or a
bilateral partnership — its own area of the Reporting Tool, with its own workspace, project
catalog, result creator, and result editor, separate from the module that handles pooled/core
(W1/W2) reporting. This guide walks a **Result submitter** — Center staff who report bilateral
results — through the manual reporting path end to end: opening the Center workspace, finding
your project, starting a result through the setup drawer, choosing how you want to report it,
completing the manual form, and working through the result editor from Save draft to the AI
quality check and Submit for review. It closes by explaining when the AI-Assisted path is the
better choice instead.

Every section pairs its narrative with a screenshot from the running Reporting Tool. Wherever
you see an orange ring around a control, that ring marks the element the section is describing;
where the ring carries a small labeled chip, the chip names that element directly. Both markers
are added only for this guide and never appear in the application itself.

To begin, sign in to PRMS with your CGIAR account at https://reporting.cgiar.org. The guide is
meant to be read in order — each section builds on the one before it, so working through it once
mirrors working through an actual submission.
