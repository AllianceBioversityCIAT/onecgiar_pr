**Evidence** requires at least one entry with a valid link before the section is complete — that
requirement is stated on screen, and the section stays flagged until it is met. **Add evidence**
opens a form for one entry at a time: a **Source of the evidence**, then either a **Link** (the
part the section actually requires) or, if you choose to upload instead, whether the evidence can
be shared publicly followed by the file itself, and finally an optional description. Each
entry you add appears as its own card, with its own edit and delete controls, up to a limit of 6
evidence entries per result; once you reach that limit, **Add evidence** disappears and the section
shows "Maximum of 6 evidence items reached."
