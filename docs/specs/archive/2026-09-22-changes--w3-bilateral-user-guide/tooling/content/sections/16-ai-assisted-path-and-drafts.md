Choosing **AI-Assisted** in the setup drawer does not open a form at all — as covered earlier in
this guide, it replaces the drawer with a document-upload panel instead. From there you attach
the documents, previously reported data, or other supporting evidence you already have, and PRMS
processes them in the background: a status panel checks progress periodically, and you do not
have to keep it open while it works — a persistent chip in the Center header shows a job is still
running and how long it has been running, so you can leave and come back.

When processing finishes, the panel confirms a draft is ready, tells you to review it in **AI
Draft Results** and promote the ones you want to report, and offers a **Go to Draft Results**
button; the draft itself is marked as generated with AI assistance from your sources, to be
reviewed and edited before submitting. The same **AI Draft Results** tab in the Center header
opens the same list directly.

**AI Draft Results** — also called **My Drafts** from inside a draft's own screen — lists every
draft an upload has produced for the Center, each carrying the fields the AI extracted from your
documents, next to two actions: **Create Result** and **Discard**. Clicking **Create Result**
opens a dialog summarizing the draft's Science Program, project, type, and Center, and its own
Create Result button stays disabled until you tick a required confirmation — *"I confirm that my
Center has reviewed and validated this AI-generated draft and its source evidence"* — this is not
a plain yes/no, it is an attestation about your Center's own review. Confirming turns the draft
into a real bilateral result, pre-filled with the extracted data, and opens it in the same result
editor this guide has already walked through — from there it is the same Save draft, AI quality
check, and Submit for review already covered. **Discard** asks a simpler confirmation and
permanently deletes the draft and everything extracted with it; it does not ask for any
attestation, only that you mean to delete — and it cannot be undone.

**When to prefer each.** Complete the Form Manually is the direct path this guide has documented
end to end: you enter every field yourself, with nothing to review or correct afterward. AI-
Assisted is worth choosing instead when you already have documents or previously reported data to
draw from and would rather review and correct an extracted draft than type each field by hand.
Either way, once a result exists — typed by hand or promoted from a draft — it is the same editor,
the same sections, and the same path to Submit for review.
