The Reporting module in PRMS enables CGIAR Programs and Accelerators to submit annual results,
track progress against planned deliverables and maintain an up-to-date overview of their
performance throughout the reporting cycle.

Every section pairs its narrative with a real screenshot from the Reporting Tool. Wherever you
see an orange ring or arrow, it marks the single button, link, or item that section is talking
about — that marker is added only for this guide and never appears in the real application.

To begin, sign in to PRMS with your CGIAR account at https://reporting.cgiar.org. For testing
and training, users are invited to use the testing environment at https://reporting-test.cgiar.org.
Once you are signed in, the sections that follow move in the same order you will naturally work
through them: landing, overview, reporting, results, notifications, and innovation packages.
