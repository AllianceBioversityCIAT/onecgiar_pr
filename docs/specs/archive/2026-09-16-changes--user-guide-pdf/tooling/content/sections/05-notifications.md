Notifications is where other Programs, Accelerators, or Window 3/Bilateral projects reach out
to you about shared results. The **Requests** tab lists collaboration requests — for example,
another Program asking to be added as a contributor to one of your results — while the
**Updates** tab covers other changes worth knowing about. Use **Requests received** to see what
has come in for you, or **Requests sent** to check on requests you sent to others, and the phase
filter (for example **Reporting 2026 - (Open)**) to scope the list to the current cycle.

This page matters because these requests affect your Program's Theory of change (ToC):
accepting one links that result to your own ToC indicators and targets, so leaving a request
pending can leave a real contribution unrecorded.

Each pending request is its own card describing who is asking and why. Open the first card in
the **Pending** list and choose **Accept contribution** to confirm it, or **Decline
contribution** if it doesn't apply to your Program.
