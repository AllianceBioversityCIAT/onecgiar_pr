Once you open a Program, its Overview page is where you land. It summarizes that Program's
reporting cycle in one screen: total results split between **W1/W2** (Windows 1 and 2,
pooled/core funding) and **W3/Bilateral** (Window 3, bilateral funding), how many CGIAR Centers
are contributing, and progress against the Program's planned Key Performance Indicators (KPIs),
grouped by Area of Work (AoW).

This page matters because it is where you judge how far a Program has come before drilling into
any single area — the **Progress by area of work** panel shows exactly which AoWs still have
KPIs left to report and which are further along, so you know where to focus next.

The tabs above this panel (**Overview**, **Reporting**, **Results**, and more) are how you move
between reviewing a Program's current state and going to report on it — that next step is the
**Reporting** tab, covered next.

To move from reviewing progress to entering results, click **Continue reporting**.
