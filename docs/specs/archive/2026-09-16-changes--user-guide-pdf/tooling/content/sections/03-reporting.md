The Reporting tab breaks down exactly what your Program needs to report this cycle, organized
by Area of Work (AoW). Chips above the list scope it to one kind of result — Knowledge Product,
Innovation Development, Policy Change, Innovation Use, or Capacity Sharing — and you can also
filter to **Only pending** items or mark ones you check often as **Favorites**. Use **Expand all** in the toolbar to open every AoW at once (press it again to
collapse), or an individual AoW card's own **Expand all** to open just that one. If you have
drilled into a single AoW, **All Areas of Work** takes you back to the full list.

This page matters because it turns a Program's entire reporting workload into one navigable
list instead of a single long form — you can jump straight to Market Intelligence, Accelerated
Breeding, or any other AoW your Program tracks, see how many Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
still need attention, and open just that group.

Each AoW expands into High Level Outputs (for example, "Steer to impact") that group its
indicators — opening one shows you the specific work behind that AoW's numbers.

Not sure where a given result belongs? Click **Where to report** in the page header — it opens
a guide to the right place to report. To open one Area of Work directly instead, use **By AOW**
on its row.
