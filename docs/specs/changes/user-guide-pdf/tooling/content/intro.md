The Reporting Tool is where Program and Accelerator (P/A) staff plan, submit, and track the
results of their work over a CGIAR reporting cycle — from day-to-day results entries, to
reviewing Innovation Packages, to responding to collaboration requests from other Programs.

This guide is written for P/A staff who are new to the Reporting Tool, or who want a quick
refresher on where things live. No experience with earlier CGIAR reporting systems is assumed.

You can read this guide start to finish the first time, or jump straight to the section you
need using the table of contents. Each of the six sections below covers one place in the
Reporting Tool: what you will find there, why it matters to your reporting, and the one action
worth trying first.

Every section pairs its narrative with a real screenshot from the Reporting Tool. Wherever you
see an orange ring or arrow, it marks the single button, link, or item that section is talking
about — that marker is added only for this guide and never appears in the real application.

To begin, sign in to the Reporting Tool with your CGIAR account. Once you are signed in, the
sections that follow move in the same order you will naturally work through them: landing,
overview, reporting, results, notifications, and innovation packages.
