After you sign in, the landing page is the first thing you see. Its **Welcome** banner names
the reporting phase that is currently open, so you always know which cycle your work belongs to.

Below the banner, the page lists two entry points into reporting: **My CGIAR Centers**, one
card per Center you belong to, and **My Science Programs/Accelerators**, one card per Program
you have access to — each Program card shows how many results exist for it this phase and a
progress bar for how much is still being edited versus already submitted. Alongside them, the
**Reporting overview** panel totals those numbers across all of your Programs and breaks them
down by status, so you can see at a glance how close your whole portfolio is to done.

This page matters because it is your starting point every time you sign in — it tells you where
reporting stands before you open any one Center's or Program's details.

To move from this landing page into a specific reporting flow, click **Report** on the first
card under **My CGIAR Centers** — opening a Program card works the same way. Either path leads
into that entity's own reporting details, covered next.
