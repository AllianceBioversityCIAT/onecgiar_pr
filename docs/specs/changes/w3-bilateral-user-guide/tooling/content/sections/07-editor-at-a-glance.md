Creating the result opens the result editor: a two-column layout with a **sections rail** on the
left and the scrolling form, with its own footer, on the right. Although Overview heads the
rail's list, the editor does not open there — it opens on **General information** instead.

The rail lists the result's sections in order: Overview, General information, Contributors &
partners, Geographic location, and Evidence, plus, for most result types, Type-specific details —
that last one does not appear at all for an Other Outcome or Other Output result. Each item in
the rail shows whether it still has unsaved edits, is complete, or is still pending. Above the
list, once a result is loaded, the rail also shows the result's code, type, and status; below the
list, a progress bar and a label reading **"N of M sections complete"** track how many of the
tracked sections are done.

At the bottom of the content column, a footer stays in place as you scroll. Its left side holds
**Back** and **Next** buttons to step between sections, next to a position indicator reading
"Section X of Y" for the section currently open — a different count from the rail's "sections
complete" progress, since this one tracks where you are, not how much is done. Its right side
shows the open section's own save state and a **Save draft** button; what those states mean is
covered in the next part of this guide.
