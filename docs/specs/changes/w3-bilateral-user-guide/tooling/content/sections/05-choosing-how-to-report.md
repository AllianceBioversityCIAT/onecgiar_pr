With a primary Science Program set, the drawer's second step asks you to choose how you would
like to create and report your bilateral result in PRMS. Two cards present the choice side by
side.

**AI-Assisted** is described as a way to reuse information without duplicating effort: it lets
you leverage existing result information, uploaded documents, or previously reported data, and
uses intelligent recommendations to accelerate creating the result. **Complete the Form
Manually** is described as creating a bilateral result step by step by manually completing the
reporting form. Picking **AI-Assisted** replaces the drawer's contents with a document-upload
panel in place of the form; picking **Complete the Form Manually** opens the form described next.

Until a primary Science Program is selected in the previous step, both cards stay disabled and
selecting either one returns you to that step instead. This guide documents the manual path, so
from here it follows **Complete the Form Manually** through the form and the editor. The
AI-Assisted path — and where its output later shows up, in the AI Draft Results tab — is
covered later in this guide.
