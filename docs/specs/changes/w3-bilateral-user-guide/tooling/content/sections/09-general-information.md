This is where the editor actually opens — **General information**, not Overview. It carries the
result's core minimum-data fields: a required **Title of Result**, capped at 30 words and tracked
by a word gauge as you type; a required **Description of Result**, capped at 300 words; and a
required lead contact person for the result. Below these, a **Complete full metadata** toggle
reveals additional, optional fields — including Impact Area Scores — for reporters who want to
record more than the minimum standard asks for.

In this and every other field-carrying section (Contributors & partners, Geographic location,
Evidence, and Type-specific details), an AI quality-check flag can appear once an assessment
exists — here, next to the title and next to the section as a whole — pointing at whichever field
the check found something to say about, together with the AI's own comment on it.
