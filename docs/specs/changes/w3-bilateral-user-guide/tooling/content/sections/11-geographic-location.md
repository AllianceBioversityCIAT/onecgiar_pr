**Geographic location** opens with a required choice: **What is the geographic focus of the
result?**, answered with one of five radio options — Global, Regional, Country, Sub-national, or
"This is yet to be determined." Choosing **Global** or **"This is yet to be determined"** asks
nothing further. Choosing **Regional** opens the required **Select regions** multi-select directly,
and also asks a separate yes/no question — whether there are countries you wish to specify for the
result — which, if you answer yes, opens the required **Select countries** multi-select as well.
Choosing **Country** or **Sub-national** skips straight to the required **Select countries**
multi-select, with no yes/no question on either axis; for **Sub-national**, once you have selected
at least one country, a further picker appears for each country selected, asking you to specify at
least one sub-national location within it.

For some result types, a further question asks whether the result may also have potential impact in
other geographic areas. Answering yes opens a second geographic-focus choice for that additional
scope — the same options as above, minus "This is yet to be determined," so four rather than five.
None of these four asks a yes/no follow-up: Regional opens its own required regions multi-select
directly, Country or Sub-national opens its own required countries multi-select directly, and
Sub-national again adds its own per-country picker once at least one country is selected.
