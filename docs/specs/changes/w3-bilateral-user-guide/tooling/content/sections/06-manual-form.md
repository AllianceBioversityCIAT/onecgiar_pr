The manual form asks for a handful of things in order, revealing each next step only once the
one before it is answered.

First, **Select Result Level**: **Outcome** ("Long-term changes resulting from the research") or
**Output** ("Products, services or capacities delivered"). Choosing a level reveals the **Result
Type** dropdown, scoped to that level. Outcome offers Policy Change, Innovation Use, and Other
Outcome; Output offers Capacity Sharing for Development, Knowledge Product, Innovation
Development, and Other Output — seven result types in total.

If you choose **Knowledge Product**, a repository section appears with two tabs: **Browse
repositories**, which lets you pick an existing item from a repository — once picked, the item
shows as selected (with a **Change item** option to pick a different one) and its title fills the
result's title field — and **Manual entry**, where you paste a repository link or handle and
click **Sync** to validate it.

Once a result type is chosen, the **Result title** field appears — it does not exist on the form
before that point. As you type, a word gauge next to the label counts your words against a limit
of 30, and turns to indicate you are over it. At the same time, the form checks your title
against existing results in the background and shows one of a few states beneath it: that the
check is still running, that an identical title already exists and must be changed before you can
create the result, that the check itself failed and should be retried, or that no exact match was
found and you can proceed. If PRMS finds titles that are similar but not identical, it lists them
underneath as well, for you to review before continuing.

Filling in the level, type, title, and — for a Knowledge Product — its repository handle is what
the form needs before the result can be created and the editor opens, covered next.
