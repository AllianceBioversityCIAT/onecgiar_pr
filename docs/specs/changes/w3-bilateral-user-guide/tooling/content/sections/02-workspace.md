Opening a Center from the bilateral module lands you on its workspace. Across the top sits the
**Center identity band**: the Center's acronym as the page heading, its full name underneath,
and an information button that opens a short popover describing the Center and the tab you are
currently on.

Directly below the identity band, a row of **four tabs** — **Overview**, **Reporting**,
**Results**, and **AI Draft Results** — switches between the Center's whole view of its bilateral
work. **Overview** summarizes the Center's reporting progress with a deck of KPI cards.
**Reporting** is the working tab: it opens the project catalog, covered next, where you find a
project and start a result. **Results** lists the Center's bilateral results, submitted and still
in progress, with filters — including by reporting phase — to narrow the list. **AI Draft
Results** lists results an AI process has drafted from supporting evidence, as cards you review
before deciding whether to carry one into an official submission.

The tabs stay in place as you move between them, so you can check overall progress on Overview,
jump into Reporting to start or continue a result, and come back to check Results or AI Draft
Results without losing your place in the Center.
