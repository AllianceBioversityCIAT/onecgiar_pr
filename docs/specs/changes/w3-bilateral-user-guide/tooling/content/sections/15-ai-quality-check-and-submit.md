**Submit for review**, on the section rail, stays disabled until every tracked section reports
complete, no request from this flow is already in progress, and the result is still open to editing
by your Center. Beneath the button, a short **submit note** reads "The AI quality check runs
first" — hovering or clicking it opens the fuller explanation: clicking Submit for review checks
the result's metadata for QA conformity first, and you can still adjust the data to address any
comments before the result actually reaches the Program for review.

Clicking **Submit for review** does not submit the result by itself. If any section still has
unsaved changes, or a field is filled in but over its word limit, you are told which and asked to
fix it first; otherwise, the click starts the AI quality check and a window opens over the editor
while it runs, explaining what each verdict color means — green (meets the quality criteria), amber
(acceptable, but not the best version), red (does not meet the criteria), and grey (not evaluated,
nothing to assess). Once the check finishes, the same window shows an overall verdict alongside a
per-section breakdown — each with its own color, and, where the AI has feedback, a way to open it
or to jump straight to the section it concerns — plus a note for each piece of evidence the check
looked at.

From there, the window's footer offers **Make adjustments**, which closes it and returns you to
editing. Alongside it, as long as the verdict you are looking at still matches your latest edits,
sits a second button — labeled **Submit for review**, or **Submit without quality check** if the
service could not complete the assessment — and that is the one action that actually moves the
result out of Editing and into Pending review. If you have changed the result since a stored
assessment ran, that second button is not offered: the window tells you the check predates your
latest edits and only lets you close it, so you run the check again before you can submit.

If a result already carries an assessment from an earlier check, this same rail carries a short
summary of it above Submit for review, so you do not have to rerun the check just to see its last
verdict — reopening the full window from there reads the stored result rather than requesting a
new one, and is subject to the same up-to-date requirement before it will let you submit.
